Privacy & security

Your clients' data stays your clients' data.

Built for people bound by confidentiality: CPAs, attorneys and medical practices. Here's exactly what we do, in plain English.

OAuth, never passwords

You sign in on each app's own screen. MetaWurks never stores or shares your passwords, and tokens are stored encrypted.

Encrypted, on Google Cloud

Sensitive data is encrypted, traffic runs over HTTPS, and the platform is hosted on Google Cloud.

Lock the sensitive projects

Keep a project like "Tax File Return" private and locked, apart from the rest of your workspace.

Drafts wait for your OK

Agents write the email. You read it, tweak it and approve it, and only then does it go out via Gmail.

Patient details masked

Patient identifiers are masked at intake, so the agent can work the file without exposing who it's about.

Leave clean. Never sold.

Disconnect a tool and its token is revoked. Delete your account and your data goes with it. We never sell it.

Before you trust us

The questions you should ask any AI vendor.

Straight answers to what careful firms ask first. Anything else, bring it to a 1:1 call.

Is my clients' data used to train AI models?

We never sell your data. Whether a model provider can learn from your data depends on its terms. We're confirming those terms in writing for every provider behind Auto and will walk you through them on your call.

Can MetaWurks email a client without me?

No. Email drafts wait for your approval before anything goes out through Gmail. You can edit, approve or delete each one. Anything flagged "Needs review" waits for you too.

What if the AI gets a number or a date wrong?

It can. That's why every answer cites the file it came from and agents flag what needs review. Check the source in seconds, and treat the output as prep for your professional judgment.

Can we use it with patient information? Will you sign a BAA?

Talk to us before uploading patient records. We don't claim HIPAA compliance today, and we'll state our BAA position plainly on a 1:1 call. Don't process PHI until that's settled in writing.

How does this fit ABA Opinion 512 or IRC Section 7216?

Your professional duties stay yours, and your firm sets its own client-consent policy. MetaWurks gives you controls that help: private connections, locked projects, cited answers and email drafts you approve. Check with your ethics or compliance adviser.

Do we have to replace QuickBooks, Xero, Outlook or Gmail?

No. MetaWurks works on top of the tools you already use. Connecting one is a sign-in, not a project, and no IT team is needed. You can disconnect any tool anytime.

How is this different from ChatGPT or Copilot?

General chatbots are built for everyone. MetaWurks is built for firms like yours. It connects to your firm's tools, answers from your own documents with sources, and runs agents built for tax prep, month-end close and collections, all in one private workspace.

What happens to our data if we leave?

Disconnect any tool and its access token is revoked. Delete your account and its associated data is deleted too. Your data is never sold, before or after you leave.
